# Capture and triage: no inbox, only views — structure and content as independent completions

- **Status:** adopted — interviewed, drafted, and adopted 2026-07-23; the triage flag decided the same day as a [lifecycle](lifecycle.md) amendment. The cadence-is-config stance stays marked provisional until the capture-ritual trial verdict (~2026-07-29) confirms it — see Notes.
- **Decided / updated:** 2026-07-23
- **Layer:** both — the substrate defines the capture exemption, the triage signals, and the primitive verbs; surfaces build views over the signals and run the sweep.
- **Config vs. invariant:** *Invariant* — capture is **never lossy** (no schema, lint, or commit gate between thought and capture); capture and filing are **separate acts**; **structure-completeness and content-completeness are independent, separately queryable properties**; triage-queue membership is **substrate data**, queryable by any surface, never UI-private state; triage mutations go through **substrate operations**; promotion **disposes of the capture and leaves a pointer** — never a silent loss, never a lingering duplicate; **mechanical structure-work is automatable, semantic judgment is human**. *Config-point* — the roster of capture surfaces (device/modality bindings); the view definitions (including the one that used to be called "the inbox"); composite triage verbs ("macros"); triage cadence and driver (ritual / browsing / agent mix); per-type birth states; the named routing targets (task manager, reading queue).

> **Boundary.** The [lifecycle](lifecycle.md) PDR owns the *state model*; this PDR owns the *process* (capture → triage → file) and consumes that model. The one place this PDR pressed on the state model — a triage marker orthogonal to the status axis — was decided as a **lifecycle amendment** (2026-07-23), where the flag now lives; this PDR consumes it. Upstream of the vault, this PDR is deliberately silent: it begins where a capture lands in the vault; external capture queues are named integrations (see Surfaces).

## 👁️ Intent

Thoughts arrive at the worst possible times — mid-conversation, on a phone, in someone else's meeting. A knowledge system that demands filing at the moment of capture loses the thought or trains the user to stop capturing. But a system that only captures becomes a landfill: unfiled scraps mixed indistinguishably into the corpus erode the trust that makes the corpus worth consulting. The user needs both ends held: capture that costs nothing at the moment of thought, and a later, deliberate act that pays the structure debt — with the system accounting for every capture in between, so nothing is lost and nothing masquerades as finished.

## 🎨 Design

**Capture is exempt and lossless.** Nothing stands between a thought and its capture — no required fields, no validation, no commit gate. A gate at capture time silently discards thoughts (a failed lint that eats a capture is worse than no lint); the schema's demands are deferred, not waived, and the debt is *marked* rather than enforced away. Capture and filing are two separate acts: capture is instant and unfiled by default; filing is batched and deliberate. Where captures physically accumulate — a staging folder, a scratch queue — is a mechanic of this exemption, not a product concept: per [note-types](note-types.md), location carries no meaning, and the product idea is *schema exemption until triage*, not any particular place.

**There is no inbox — there are views.** The product does not ship a privileged "inbox". It ships a cheap way to define **views** over substrate signals — lifecycle state, flags, dates, tags — so "Needs triage" sits beside "Drafts", "Starred", and "This week" as just another saved lens. What makes this work is that queue membership is **substrate data**: any surface (or agent) can ask "what awaits triage?" and get the same answer, because the answer is carried by the notes, not by one tool's private list. A dedicated inbox is the degenerate case — one view, hard-coded.

**Structure and content are independent completions.** A note is done in two separate senses: *organized* — it has the structure (type, tags, links, a clean lint) — and *current* — it has the content (the writing is finished; in lifecycle terms, neither draft nor archived). These move independently: a deliberately slow-cooking draft can be fully organized (it needs writing, not triage — it does not belong in the triage view), and a capture whose text is complete can be entirely unstructured (it needs triage, not writing — calling it a "draft" mislabels it). Collapsing the two onto one marker breaks both cases.

- **Decided as a [lifecycle](lifecycle.md) amendment (2026-07-23):** "needs triage" is a **flag orthogonal to the status axis** — by lifecycle's own litmus it is flag-shaped, since it can co-occur with draft, current, or archived. Polarity follows lifecycle's absence-is-ordinary principle: the flag marks the *exception* (unfiled captures carry it; triage clears it; absence means organized), so the mature corpus stays unmarked. Capture surfaces stamp the flag at birth. The incumbent substrate carries the flag as of the same day; birth-stamping awaits the per-type birth-state table below.

**Triage supplies structure, then disposes of the capture.** Triage is the act of paying the structure debt: give the capture its type and tags, link it, route it, clear the flag. When a capture is *promoted* — becomes or merges into a real note — one uniform rule governs the original: **dispose of it, leave a pointer.** Scratch queues empty out (the capture is deleted or completed, with a pointer to the note it became); a durable log keeps its line — the log *is* a record — but the line gains a link to the minted note and thereby becomes its own pointer. What is disposed is the *queue membership*, never the trail: promotion must never silently lose what was captured, and must never leave the capture lingering as a shadow duplicate of the note.

**The product owes primitive verbs; composites are configuration.** The substrate provides atomic triage operations — set lifecycle state, tag, clear the triage flag, mint a note from a capture — and every surface's triage goes through them, never through hand-edited state. It owes no move or rename verbs (location carries no meaning) and no cross-system routing verbs (routing targets are integrations, not core). User-defined **composite verbs — "macros"** — are a config-point any surface may offer: one gesture chaining several primitives. The primitives are the durable contract; the composites are personal workflow, which varies per person and per season.

**Triage decomposes along an automation gradient.** Part of the structure debt is mechanical — normalize a title or slug, satisfy a lint rule — and part is semantic judgment — what type is this, what does it link to, is it worth keeping. The product automates what it can mechanize and reserves the human for what it cannot; the triage surface must therefore expose the *signals* separately (is it structured? is the content complete? does it lint?) so both the automation and the human can see exactly which debt remains.

**Types may declare a birth state.** Whether a note enters life as a draft, flagged for triage, or fully current is a property its *type* may declare — a capture type is born flagged, a templated long-form type may be born organized-but-draft, a record type born current. The product stance is only that birth states are per-type declarations; the actual table is config.

**Cadence and driver are configuration.** Whether the triage view is drained by a daily ritual, by opportunistic browsing, or by a dispatched agent whose dispositions the user confirms — and on what rhythm — is a per-person, per-season choice. The invariant underneath is only that the queue is *queryable by all three*, so any mix can be configured without the substrate caring who shows up. *(Provisional: a live trial of ritual-anchored triage reports back imminently; if unforced queues rot in practice, a stronger stance than "config" may be warranted — see Notes.)*

## ⚖️ Tradeoffs

- **Rejected: a first-class inbox.** One privileged "inbox" concept — whether a single unioned lens over every capture feed, or a dedicated place with its own semantics — enshrines one workflow as architecture. The union version forces heterogeneous items (files, tasks, log lines) into a lowest-common-denominator lens and demands cross-system reads; the dedicated-place version contradicts location-carries-no-meaning and duplicates what a view already does. A view system delivers the inbox *and* every sibling lens ("Starred", "This week") for the same price, and the inbox stops being special — which is the point: triage is a lens you sweep, not a room you maintain.
- **Rejected: schema-gated capture.** Requiring conformance at capture time — pick a type, pass the lint, commit clean — loses thoughts at exactly the moments capture matters most, and teaches the user to route around the system. Deferred structure with a marked, queryable debt beats structure-at-birth everywhere the human isn't at a full keyboard with attention to spare.
- **Rejected: "needs triage" = the draft state.** Reusing not-finished-writing as not-yet-filed conflates two independent completions. The tell is that the failure modes point in opposite directions: the slow-cooking essay clogs a triage queue it doesn't belong in, while the finished-but-unfiled capture wears a "draft" label that lies about its content. One marker cannot serve two axes that move independently.
- **Rejected: captures preserved forever.** Archiving every promoted capture next to the note it became doubles the bookkeeping and litters the corpus with shadow copies whose only content is "I used to be this note." The note is the record; a pointer preserves the provenance. Equally rejected in the other direction: silent deletion — disposal without a pointer breaks the trail from "I know I captured this" to where it went.
- **Rejected: routing as core product behavior.** "Task-shaped goes to a task manager, reading goes to a reading queue" is real practice, but making it first-class drags named external systems into the product's core and makes the pipeline's correctness depend on integrations it doesn't control. The pipeline starts where a capture lands in the vault; upstream queues and routing targets are integrations named in configuration.
- **Rejected: one composite "promote" gesture as the product's verb.** An interactive do-everything pass (status + tags + links + filing in one gesture) matches how triage often *feels*, but baking it in makes the bundle the contract — and the bundle is the part that varies per person. Primitives compose into anyone's gesture; a gesture doesn't decompose back into anyone's primitives.
- **Tension carried, not resolved:** with cadence as config, no forcing function is guaranteed — a queue nobody sweeps rots silently. The product makes the debt *visible* (queryable from every surface), not *self-paying*. Whether visibility without ritual suffices is exactly what the live trial reports on (see Notes).

## 🖥️ Surfaces

The signals and verbs are substrate; each surface builds its own lens over them.

- **CLI / SDK (capture + authoring).** A capture verb writes with zero gates — no tags, no commit, lint-exempt by design. The primitive triage verbs ship as substrate mutation verbs (metadata mutations go through the verbs, never hand-written frontmatter) — validated, lint-gated, pathspec-scoped commits.
- **TUI (the view surface).** Saved views over substrate signals are the browse model; the triage view keys on the flag (with the draft state as a transitional second arm while the pre-flag draft queue drains), and triage actions (status picker, tag editor, flag toggle) go through the verbs. Macros — user-defined composite triage verbs — are a config surface here; their concrete shape is a [Product](../../product.md) design question, not a product invariant.
- **MCP.** The triage flag and lifecycle state are filter + return fields, so a remote agent can pull the queue, do the mechanical share of the work, and present dispositions for confirmation — the agent-driver leg of the cadence config.
- **GUI editor (fallback).** The prior art: the incumbent fallback editor's Inbox coupled to the draft state and carried a second `_organized` axis — evidence for the two-property model, with the polarity inverted (it marked the achievement; the amendment marks the exception).
- **Mechanics of the capture exemption (the incumbent corpus, today — all config):** the capture-surface roster by device/modality (a hotkey into the day note's log, a task-manager scratch queue on mobile, a pre-schema staging folder for file drops — version-ignored and linter-excluded, the exemption made literal — and a reading queue); external queues are drained into the vault by ritual or agent, and views take over from there.
