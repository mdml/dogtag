Quince day. [[Milo Farrant]] arrived at the plot with two crates and the expression of a man who has already given quinces to everyone else he knows, so the annual arithmetic has begun: two crates in, one [[Quince and Rye Galette]] out, net position worsening.

Baked the first galette of the season in the evening and it held — the rye crust shattered properly, the syrup glaze took, and the kitchen smelled like October arrived early. Took half of it back to the shed for whoever opens up on Monday.

The allotment stream is the lowest I have seen it this early in the summer, which is either a dry month or plate 7 acquiring a subplot. Paced the exposed culvert apron and made a strip entry for it; the desk notebook can argue about what it means next week.
