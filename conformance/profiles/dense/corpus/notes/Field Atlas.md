Before it had a name: an atlas of the Hollow's edges, printed properly, one plate per edge. The surveyed line and the [[Drift Mapping]] ribbon on the same sheet, so the map argues with itself. Eight edges came to mind on the walk back and I have deliberately not edited the list since — winch platform, saltmarsh channels, the dry channel, the shingle bands, the harbour mouth, the culvert, the point itself, and the whole Hollow at once.
