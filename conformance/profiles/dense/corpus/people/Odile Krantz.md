# Odile Krantz

Publishes the early notebook pieces under the Fenwick name, which took me an embarrassing year to work out. We met at the paper fair the winter before last; she was the only person at the ephemera table reading the backs of the maps instead of the fronts.

Generous with scans of her collection, slow with everything else. Write first, and write short.
