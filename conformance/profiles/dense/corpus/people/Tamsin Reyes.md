# Tamsin Reyes

Met at the cooperative's open-press evening, arguing amiably with the intaglio people about drying racks. She has since talked me into the membership, out of two bad paper choices, and into printing the atlas plates at 300 by 400 rather than the folio size I could not have afforded.

Keeps a plot at the allotment as well, three rows down from Milo's, mostly given over to dye plants. If a message goes unanswered for a week she is at the coast; try the studio pinboard instead.
