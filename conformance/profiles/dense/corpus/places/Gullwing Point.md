# Gullwing Point

Headland at the mouth of the Hollow, reached by the ridge path or, at low water, along the shingle. The bench above the old winch platform is the driest place to draw on the whole coast.

The point loses a little ground every winter; the winch platform photographs from 2019 already show a different edge than the one I walk now. This is the anchor site for the first atlas plate and the place I test every drift-mapping idea before trusting it anywhere else.
