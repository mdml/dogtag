# Brindlewood Cooperative

Two etching presses, one proofing press, and a paper store that smells like every good decision I have made since joining. Membership renews in March; the key-holder rota is the only bureaucracy anyone enforces.

Open-press evenings first Thursday of the month, which is where most of the atlas's practical problems have actually been solved.
